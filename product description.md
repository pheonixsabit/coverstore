## 🎯 Conversion Optimization Tips for Product Listings

1. **Include Real Photos & Video B-Roll:**  
   Always include real-life angle photos showing the raised camera ring, button tactile clicks, and port cutouts alongside lifestyle images.
2. **Highlight the Fear of Screen Replacement:**  
   Remind customers that replacing an AMOLED display or rear camera glass costs 10,000–35,000 BDT, while an ultra-premium cover costs only a fraction of that.
3. **Keep Color Options Clear:**  
   Use standardized color names (Classic Black, Antique White, Lavender Gray, Royal Blue, etc.) matching the custom SKU tags to prevent buyer confusion.
4. **Transparent Risk-Reversal:**  
   Clearly mention: *"You can inspect the product in front of the delivery agent before paying!"* This single line boosts conversion rates by over 40% in Bangladesh e-commerce.
